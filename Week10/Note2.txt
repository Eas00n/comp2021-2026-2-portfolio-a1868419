1. If I spend 10 minutes on a 10-mark question and I am only halfway finished, I will stop and move to the next question. I will first make sure I have written down anything I know about the question. If I still have time after answering the other questions, I will come back and finish it. I do not want to spend too much time on one question and lose marks from other questions.

2. I will first check the question again and see if I really cannot answer anything else. If I think my answer is enough, I will move to the next question. If I can add a few useful points, I will add them and try to get full marks. If I am not sure, I will continue to the next question first and come back later. This will help me avoid spending too much time on one question.

3. I will quickly look through the remaining questions and answer the ones I can do quickly first. For example, if I have 15 minutes left, I will try to put something useful for both questions instead of spending all 15 minutes on one question. If I finish the basic answers and still have time, I will go back and improve them. I think it is better to attempt every question because leaving a question blank gives me no chance to get marks.
