2.An exception was thrown.
  This happened because the score is the key in the SortedDictionary and keys must be unique.

3.SortedSet stores only values and keeps them sorted.
  SortedDictionary stores key-value pairs and keeps them sorted by key.
  A SortedSet could be used for a leaderboard containing unique scores.
  A SortedDictionary could be used for player rankings where each score is linked to a player name.
